# Brand Profile: Serenity Scrolls

This profile defines the identity, voice, assets, and keywords for **Serenity Scrolls** (`serenityscrolls.faith`). The swarm uses this context to generate meta tags, schema markup, sitemaps, FAQs, and email outreach copy.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Voice

* **Core Essence**: Scripture scrolls, devotional tools, prayer journals, and the Courage Covenant parenting study curriculum designed to cultivate anxiety-relief, peace, and healthy family boundaries.
* **Mantra**: "Sow in Serenity, Reclaim Your Courage."
* **Product Line**:
  * **Bible Verse Scrolls**: Handcrafted wooden boxes with scroll capsules containing selected scriptures for anxiety, peace, strength, and joy.
  * **Reflection & Prayer Journals**: Hardcover guides for personal morning quiet times.
  * **Courage Covenant LMS**: Digital parenting course offering framework modules on biblical boundaries, child boundary exits, and parental reconciliation.
* **Product Copy Rules**:
  * Warm, reflective, spiritually encouraging, and biblically sound. Avoid overly corporate or dry language.
  * Use: **"Cultivating quiet hearts and courageous families, one daily scripture at a time."**
  * Block: Do NOT use overly clinical mental health terminology without grounding it in faith-based encouragement.
* **Pricing & Badges**: Promote "Bestseller", "New Release", and bundled options (e.g., Courage Covenant + Journal).

## 4. Wholesale B2B Outreach Demographics

* **Target Markets**: Christian bookstores, independent church gift shops, local religious boutiques, retreat centers, and family ministries/church groups.
