# Courage Covenant™ Curriculum Overview
**A Scripture-Based Bullying Guidance Course for Parents, Students & Faith Leaders**

Welcome to the Courage Covenant™ resources. This document outline contains the complete structure of the 8-module, 32-lesson curriculum. Use this directory guide as a roadmap to navigate the program.

---

## 📅 Course Curriculum Roadmap

### 🧱 Module 1: What Actually Happened?
*Distinguish conflict, teasing, bullying, and danger with clear categories.*
* **Lesson 1.1: Conflict vs. Bullying: Know the Difference (Free Preview)**
  * *Focus*: Why adults minimize or overreact, and how to define key terms.
* **Lesson 1.2: The 4 Categories: Conflict, Teasing, Bullying, Danger**
  * *Focus*: Real-world examples of each category to establish clear boundaries.
* **Lesson 1.3: Understanding Power Imbalance**
  * *Focus*: Spotting structural/social imbalances and helping your child understand them without fear.
* **Lesson 1.4: Online vs. Offline: Same Framework, Different Tools**
  * *Focus*: Applying identical guidelines to digital spaces, social media exclusion, and group chats.

---

### 💬 Module 2: What Is the Child Feeling?
*Name and validate the 9 emotional responses of a targeted child.*
* **Lesson 2.1: Naming the Emotion Without Minimizing**
  * *Focus*: Learning to decrypt indirect child expressions (e.g., "I hate school") to find the root feeling.
* **Lesson 2.2: The 9 Emotions of Bullying**
  * *Focus*: Processing *Sad, Anxious, Frustrated, Troubled, Afraid, Angry, Ashamed, Confused, and Lonely*.
* **Lesson 2.3: Your Regulation Before Theirs**
  * *Focus*: A 60-second nervous system reset for parents so they can listen calmly without immediate panic.
* **Lesson 2.4: When the Body Speaks: Physical Signs of Distress**
  * *Focus*: Identifying stomach aches, sleep changes, school avoidance, and other bodily stress signs.

---

### ✝ Module 3: What Does Scripture Say?
*Anchor response in truth, courage, and protection—avoiding the "spiritual duct-tape" trap.*
* **Lesson 3.1: Scripture Does Not Deny Pain**
  * *Focus*: Honest emotion in the Psalms, Jesus' responses to harm, and correcting weaponized passivity.
* **Lesson 3.2: Comfort, Courage & Wisdom Scriptures**
  * *Focus*: Finding specific Bible verses categorized for emotional comfort, standing strong, or stepping away.
* **Lesson 3.3: What Not to Say Spiritually**
  * *Focus*: Replacing harmful clichés (e.g., "just pray and ignore them") with healthy, truth-rooted validation.
* **Lesson 3.4: Using Serenity Scrolls in This Moment**
  * *Focus*: Utilizing emotion-coded scrolls and the AI Servant to match Bible verses to specific distress states.

---

### 🗣 Module 4: What the Child Can Say
*Word-for-word verbal boundaries, exit strategies, and support scripts.*
* **Lesson 4.1: Boundary, Exit & Help-Seeking Responses**
  * *Focus*: The three essential verbal categories to prevent freezing under pressure.
* **Lesson 4.2: Boundary Scripts: "Stop. I don't like that."**
  * *Focus*: Clear, direct, non-provocative phrases to state boundaries clearly.
* **Lesson 4.3: Exit Scripts: "I'm walking away."**
  * *Focus*: Walking away confidently without feeling shame or escalating the conflict.
* **Lesson 4.4: Practice Without Pressure**
  * *Focus*: Low-stress roleplay techniques parents can use at home to build muscle memory.

---

### 🛡 Module 5: What Parents Should Do
*A step-by-step framework to document, clarify, and take appropriate action.*
* **Lesson 5.1: The First 60 Seconds**
  * *Focus*: Establishing safety instantly: *"I'm glad you told me. You are safe. We will handle this."*
* **Lesson 5.2: The parent response framework**
  * *Focus*: *Listen → Clarify → Comfort → Document → Decide*.
* **Lesson 5.3: When to Contact the School or Church**
  * *Focus*: Identifying objective criteria and draft scripts for communicating with adult leaders.
* **Lesson 5.4: Documenting Without Drama**
  * *Focus*: How to log dates, times, screenshots, and witnesses systematically to build an unassailable record.

---

### 🟢🟡🔴 Module 6: The Escalation Framework
*A clear decision map that removes confusion about when to coach, document, or act.*
* **Lesson 6.1: 🟢 Green Level: Monitor & Coach**
  * *Focus*: Handling low-conflict situations (one-time teasing, balanced disagreement).
* **Lesson 6.2: 🟡 Yellow Level: Document & Involve Adults**
  * *Focus*: Handling repeated exclusion, patterns of targeting, or digital harassment.
* **Lesson 6.3: 🔴 Red Level: Immediate Escalation**
  * *Focus*: Responding to physical safety threats, threats of self-harm, harassment, or abuse.
* **Lesson 6.4: Using the Decision Map**
  * *Focus*: Case-study practice worksheets to remove second-guessing.

---

### 🕊 Module 7: Forgiveness, Boundaries & Wisdom
*Biblical forgiveness without enabling harm. Boundaries are not bitterness.*
* **Lesson 7.1: What Forgiveness Actually Is**
  * *Focus*: Releasing vengeance to God while maintaining absolute truth and reality.
* **Lesson 7.2: What Forgiveness Is NOT**
  * *Focus*: Exposing four ways forgiveness is weaponized (denial, immediate trust, avoiding consequences).
* **Lesson 7.3: Boundaries Are Not Bitterness**
  * *Focus*: Setting loving, healthy walls of protection.
* **Lesson 7.4: Reconciliation Requires Safety First**
  * *Focus*: Distinguishing forgiveness (immediate, internal) from reconciliation (earned, relational, safe).

---

### ✨ Module 8: The 7-Day Courage Ritual
*Establish long-term daily habits that build emotional resilience.*
* **Lesson 8.1: The Daily 5-Step Courage Ritual**
  * *Focus*: Name Emotion → Draw Scroll → Read Scripture → Journal Honestly → Take One Wise Step.
* **Lesson 8.2: Using the AI Servant in Your Ritual**
  * *Focus*: Accessing the Courage Covenant mode and parent dashboard.
* **Lesson 8.3: Your 7-Day Courage Plan**
  * *Focus*: A daily checklist combining scrolls, journals, and the digital AI companion.
* **Lesson 8.4: Continuing the Ritual Beyond 7 Days**
  * *Focus*: How to carry this resilience practice forward and share it with your church or small group.
