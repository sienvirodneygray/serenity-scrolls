# 🟢🟡🔴 The Green / Yellow / Red Escalation Framework
*A clear decision-making map for families, schools, and faith communities.*

When bullying or relational conflict occurs, parents and leaders often swing between two extremes: either minimizing the behavior ("just ignore them," "kids will be kids") or overreacting immediately. 

The Courage Covenant™ framework divides situations into three distinct categories with clear, objective indicators, response pathways, and boundaries.

---

## 📊 Summary of the Framework

| Level | Classification | Key Indicators | Direct Action Pathway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🟢 Green** | **Monitor & Coach** | • One-time conflict/teasing<br>• Relational balance (no power imbalance)<br>• Child feels emotionally safe<br>• Minor physical boundaries | **Coach your child:**<br>• Listen & validate emotions<br>• Practice boundary & exit scripts<br>• Monitor relational changes |
| **🟡 Yellow** | **Document & Involve** | • Repeated relational targeting/exclusion<br>• Clear power imbalance<br>• Chronic anxiety or distress<br>• Digital harassment / online exclusion | **Involve adult leaders:**<br>• Create an objective timeline log<br>• Schedule a meeting with teachers/leaders<br>• Establish home check-ins |
| **🔴 Red** | **Immediate Escalation** | • Physical harm or threats of violence<br>• Self-harm or suicidal statements<br>• Abuse, grooming, or exploitation<br>• Severe legal/safety violations | **Stop & Protect:**<br>• Intervene immediately<br>• Involve administrators & authorities<br>• Prioritize safety over devotional study |

---

## 🟢 Green Level: Monitor & Coach
*Healthy friction is a normal part of growing up. Green level situations represent minor relational conflict or teasing without an established pattern or power imbalance.*

### Indicators
* The conflict is a one-time occurrence or happens very rarely.
* There is no severe power difference; both children are of similar age, status, or influence.
* The targeted child is upset but does not display chronic anxiety, dread, or school avoidance.
* No physical boundaries have been crossed in a dangerous or harmful way.

### Action Plan
1. **Active Listening**: Give your child full attention. Let them name their feelings using the *9 Emotions of Bullying*.
2. **Coaching**: Do not step in to fight the battle for them yet. Coach them to use **Exit** and **Boundary** scripts.
3. **Observation**: Keep a quiet eye on the situation to ensure it remains at the Green level.

---

## 🟡 Yellow Level: Document & Involve Adults
*Yellow level situations represent relational harm that has transitioned from normal friction into systemic mistreatment or repeated harassment.*

### Indicators
* **Repetition**: The behavior is ongoing (happening multiple times a week).
* **Power Imbalance**: The instigator is older, larger, holds high social power, or coordinates group exclusion.
* **Child Distress**: The child shows physical indicators of distress (headaches, stomach aches, refusing school/youth group, bedwetting, or withdrawal).
* **Cyber Issues**: Harassment is carried out through social media, messaging apps, or group chat exclusion.

### Action Plan
1. **Document Everything**: Use a systematic tracking log (Date, Time, Location, Incident details, Witnesses, Evidence).
2. **Engage Authority**: Schedule a formal, calm conversation with the school teacher, principal, youth pastor, or group leader. Present your objective log.
3. **Set Relational Rest**: Give your child permission to step away from the specific environment or group temporarily if needed.

---

## 🔴 Red Level: Immediate Escalation
*Red level situations represent direct threats to physical safety, legal rights, or emotional survival. In these moments, devotional/coaching frameworks take a back seat to direct protective intervention.*

### Indicators
* Any form of physical assault, violence, or direct threats of bodily harm.
* Statements of self-harm, hopelessness, or suicidal ideation by the child.
* Signs of abuse, grooming, severe intimidation, or sexual harassment.
* Acts that constitute clear violations of law or civil rights.

### Action Plan
1. **Immediate Intervention**: Remove your child from the environment immediately. **Do not send them back.**
2. **Contact Authorities**: Report physical safety violations to law enforcement or child protection services as appropriate.
3. **Professional Support**: Immediately secure licensed professional counseling or crisis intervention. 

> [!WARNING]
> Red-level situations are emergencies. Never rely solely on an educational course, self-guided workbook, or AI assistant. Take active, immediate, physical steps to protect the child.

---

## 📝 Practice Check-In Guide
Ask these three questions to immediately determine a situation's level:
1. **Is this repeated or a one-time event?** *(Pattern)*
2. **Is one child significantly more powerful, older, or influential?** *(Power Imbalance)*
3. **Does the child feel safe going back to that environment tomorrow?** *(Emotional/Physical Safety)*
